Reverie

privacy.

EFFECTIVE 8 NOVEMBER 2026

Your music and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves it is a record of the Reverie Full purchase, used for sales analytics.

This policy covers the Reverie apps for iPhone and Android, made by Mahad Aamir (MHD Labs).

What stays on your device

Songs you import are copied into Reverie's own storage on your phone, together with their title, artist and the tempo, pitch and reverb you set for each one. Your preferences (theme, wheel mode, shuffle and repeat) are stored the same way. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

If your phone's system backup is turned on (iCloud Backup on iPhone, or Android backup to your Google Account), the operating system may include this data in your own backup. We can't access it.

Motion sensors and vibration

Reverie reads your phone's motion or gravity sensor to move the light across the wheel as you tilt the phone. This is processed instantly on the device and is never stored or transmitted. Reverie also uses your phone's vibration motor for haptic clicks; nothing is recorded.

Playback controls

The title and artist of the song that's playing are shown by your phone's own media controls, such as the lock screen, the notification or Control Center, and connected Bluetooth devices. They aren't sent to us.

Purchases

The Reverie Full purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google through Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account or Google Account information. That data is covered by Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

To see sales as they happen, Reverie uses RevenueCat as a service provider. When you open the app or make a purchase, RevenueCat receives your purchase history for Reverie (product, price, currency, date and the store's transaction ID), a random ID the app creates, your country, and basic device details such as the OS and app version. Apple and Google also send RevenueCat a copy of the purchase. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf only to give us sales analytics. It doesn't receive your name, email address or payment details, and the data isn't linked to your identity or used for advertising or tracking.

Ratings

Reverie may ask you to rate it using the App Store's or Google Play's built-in review prompt. Your rating goes to Apple or Google; the app doesn't receive it.

No tracking

Reverie has no accounts, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. Its only network requests are the purchase records sent to RevenueCat described above.

Children

Reverie isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
