Reverie

privacy.

EFFECTIVE 2 NOVEMBER 2026

Reverie does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your music and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Songs you import are copied into Reverie's own storage on your iPhone, together with their title, artist and the tempo, pitch and reverb you set for each one. Your preferences (theme, wheel mode, shuffle and repeat) are stored the same way. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Motion sensors

Reverie reads your iPhone's motion sensors to move the light across the wheel as you tilt the phone. This is processed instantly on the device and is never stored or transmitted.

Purchases

The Reverie Full purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

No tracking

Reverie has no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs, makes no network requests of its own, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Because Reverie collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
