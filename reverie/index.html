How it works(05)
01

Spin to scrub

A brushed-metal wheel instead of a progress bar. Turn it slowly to scratch like a record, or flick it and let it spin on. Haptic clicks with every turn.

02

One wheel, every dial

Tap T, P or R and the wheel becomes a dial. Tempo from half speed to 1.5× without changing key, pitch a full octave either way, reverb from dry to a wide wash.

03

Every tape remembers

Each song keeps its own tempo, pitch and reverb. Make your slowed version once and it plays that way every time.

04

All your music, one deck

Import MP3, WAV, FLAC or M4A files, plus AIFF on iPhone and OGG on Android. On iPhone, your synced music library, Apple Music and your Navidrome server play on the deck too. No account and no tracking.

05

Beyond the app

On iPhone: Home Screen widgets, a Live Activity, Lock Screen and AirPods controls, and a layout made for iPhone Duo, folded or open.
