Common questions

How do I add songs?Tap the screen at the top of the deck to open the library, then tap ADD SONGS and choose audio files: from the Files app on iPhone, or from your phone, Google Drive or any storage app on Android. Reverie supports MP3, WAV, FLAC and M4A, plus AIFF on iPhone and OGG on Android. Songs from Apple Music, Spotify or other streaming services can't be imported.
How do I change tempo, pitch or reverb?Tap T, P or R, then turn the wheel. Hold a key to reset it. Each song remembers its own settings.
The wheel scratches. Can it scrub silently?Yes. In the library, open Settings and set Wheel to REWIND.
What does the free version include?The free plan plays the first three songs you add. Reverie Full is a one-time purchase that unlocks every song in your library.
I bought Reverie Full on another device.Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google Account (Android). A purchase on one platform doesn't carry over to the other.
How do I get a refund?Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google Play's refund steps.
How do I remove a song?In the library, touch and hold the song, then choose Remove. This deletes Reverie's copy only; the original file stays where it was.
