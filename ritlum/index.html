How it works(04)
01

Every goal, your way

Check it off, count it up or run a timer. Daily, weekly or any rhythm you like, with reminders tied to each habit.

02

Tap a tag. It's logged.

Stick an NFC tag on something you already touch, like a kettle, a book or a yoga mat, and tap it with your iPhone.

03

Ritlum mini

An 8 × 8 light grid for your desk showing your last eight days, synced over Bluetooth. No Wi-Fi and no account of its own.

04

See what's working

Charts and three-month trends for every habit, so you can tell which ones are sticking.
